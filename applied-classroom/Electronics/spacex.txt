Sourcing Manager (Launch Vehicle Electronic Systems)
Hawthorne, CA

SpaceX was founded under the belief that a future where humanity is out exploring the stars is fundamentally more exciting than one where we are not. Today SpaceX is actively developing the technologies to make this possible, with the ultimate goal of enabling human life on Mars.

SOURCING MANAGER (LAUNCH VEHICLE ELECTRONICS SYSTEMS)

RESPONSIBILITIES

    Create sourcing strategies for assigned electronics categories (PCB, PCBA/contract manufacturing, electronic components, and instrumentation) to reduce risk and meet targets including payment terms, cash, and cost savings
    Develop and manage a domestic and international supply base for those categories; communicate, manage, and drive compliance to category strategies
    Work directly with key suppliers to implement sourcing and cost-management strategies for existing platforms and new product development
    Identify and execute cost-reduction projects (piece price, yield, tooling, dual-source, obsolescence) including planning, stakeholder coordination, estimated completion, and updates
    Perform quantitative and business-process analysis of spend, capacity, technical capability, and financial impact of sourcing decisions
    Evaluate demand, place and manage purchase orders, and track supplier delivery performance
    Act as the supplier interface for all RFIs, RFPs, and RFQs; run RFX with stakeholders and implement the sourcing decision
    Collaborate with stakeholders to define SLAs and KPIs for suppliers (cost, quality, on-time delivery, lead time, responsiveness) and run programs to develop and monitor the supply base
    Provide guidance to engineering on recommended suppliers, design for manufacturability, and trade analysis for avionics hardware; strong ability to read drawings, fab notes, stack-ups, and BOMs and to understand manufacturing complexity
    Ensure suppliers are prepared to support prototype, ramp, and rate with the right process, equipment, and material
    Visit supplier facilities and assess operational capability, financial health, capacity, and risk before awarding NPI and long-term production
    Mitigate sole/single-source risk; negotiate contracts for continuity of supply and to protect SpaceX interests
    Partner with internal resources to drive low-cost part adoption and obsolescence avoidance; monitor industry and technology roadmaps with design
    Present value propositions to management and executives: contract award, cost reviews, cost-reduction strategies, and critical supply issues
    Work with material planners and supply chain engineers so demand, inventory, and supplier commitment stay aligned to the build plan — planners own MRP; this seat owns the supplier, the contract, and the cost
    Develop and maintain strong relationships with suppliers and with internal stakeholders across disciplines and levels of management

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS

Advanced degree and 3+ years of experience working in one or more of the following; or bachelor's degree and 5+ years of experience working in one or more of the following; or 9+ years of experience working in one or more of the following:

    Engineering
    Purchasing
    Contract negotiation
    Material or production planning
    Operations or supply chain management
    Material cost control
    Master production scheduling
    Technical procurement with PCB, PCBA, electronic components, or instrumentation

PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE

    Electronics industry experience; technical procurement of PCB, PCBA/contract manufacturing, electronic components, or instrumentation
    Experience reading technical drawings, fab drawings, stack-ups, data sheets, and BOMs, with understanding of avionics manufacturing complexity
    Experience in NPI and production; experience managing an existing domestic and global electronics supply base
    Track record of category strategy, cost-reduction projects, and negotiating piece price and tooling
    Experience with MRP or ERP, and with managing BOMs and purchase orders
    Exceptional analytical, problem-solving, and organizational skills; proficiency in MS Office, and SQL or PowerBI a plus
    Effective written, verbal, and presentation communication skills
    Ability to learn quickly, prioritize, and respond in a fast-moving environment; bias for speed and impact
    APICS or Lean/Six Sigma certification a plus
    Conducts all activities with the highest level of integrity
